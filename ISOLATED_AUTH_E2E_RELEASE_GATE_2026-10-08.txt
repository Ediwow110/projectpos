FINAL INVESTIGATION AND FIXES — 2026-10-08
===========================================
1. Draft creation HTTP 400 response exposed an application exception:
   Cannot read properties of undefined (reading 'role').
   Source: the split SalesController read req.auth!.role from its perms()
   helper without authenticating the request. The controller lost its
   class-level authentication guards during controller decomposition.
2. Restored @UseGuards(ApiTokenGuard, SessionGuard) on each split class:
   SalesController, PaymentsController and ReceiptsController.
   ApiTokenGuard runs first, allowing validated bearer tokens with explicit
   scopes; SessionGuard accepts the token-authenticated request or validates
   the normal session cookie.
3. Auth guard and sales tests then passed, and the browser rerun advanced
   through "Sale completed." successfully. It next failed on receipt view
   with NOT_FOUND instead of a receipt title.
4. Root cause of receipt NOT_FOUND: the two new split controllers
   PaymentsController and ReceiptsController were never added to the
   controllers arrays in apps/api/src/app.module.ts for either AppModule or
   createTestApp(). They existed in an unused SalesModule but were NOT
   reachable from the running root application.
5. Registered the split controllers in both root and test-app lists.
   Added metadata regression tests asserting guard order on all three
   controllers and registration of all three in the runtime AppModule.
6. Removed 33 unused import warnings left over in split sales, payment and
   receipt controller files, without modifying endpoint logic.
